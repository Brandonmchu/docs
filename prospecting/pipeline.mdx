## Overview

The **Sales Pipeline** is where Every turns a promising relationship into a managed sales process. Warm leads discovered by the [Prospecting Agent](/prospecting/prospecting-agent) arrive for review; approved leads get a plan, outreach drafts, linked proposals and invoices, and a history of what happened.

Every prepares and updates the work, while you control who enters the pipeline and what gets sent.

## Pipeline stages

**Pending** is a review queue. The four columns after it are the active pipeline.

| Column | What it means |
|-|-|
| **Pending** | Agent-discovered warm leads waiting for approval or rejection. |
| **Lead** | An approved prospect before a meaningful sales motion is underway. |
| **Opportunity** | An active sales motion, from a positive reply through proposal, invoice, and close. |
| **Won** | Work closed successfully under your configured criteria. |
| **Lost** | Work closed unsuccessfully or intentionally parked. |

Opportunity holds the full active conversation instead of splitting every milestone into a separate stage. Proposal and invoice status appear on the deal so you can see the commercial state without rebuilding your pipeline around each document.

## How deals get into the pipeline

- **Warm Leads:** Every searches your network and puts qualified matches in Pending.
- **Manual deals:** Add a contact or client directly to any active stage.
- **Commercial activity:** When configured, proposals or invoices created outside the board can be linked to a deal so the sales and billing records stay connected.

## Review the Pending queue

Open a Pending lead to see:

- The reason Every surfaced the person
- Fit and relationship scores
- Supporting evidence and source context
- The intended path from relationship to sale

Then choose:

- **Approve** — moves the person to Lead and asks the pipeline agent to build the first sales plan and outreach draft.
- **Reject** — removes the person and prevents that contact from being suggested in future Warm Leads searches. An optional reason helps improve future scoring.

No message is sent from Pending.

## Plans, outreach, and activity

Each approved deal has a plan made of concrete next steps. The agent refreshes that plan when relevant facts change—for example, after you log a conversation, move the stage, send outreach, receive a reply, or link a proposal or invoice.

Open a deal to:

- Review the current plan and its rationale
- Edit and send an outreach draft
- Log a call, meeting, note, or attachment
- Draft a proposal
- Link or inspect proposals and invoices
- Change the deal owner on a team workspace
- Review the chronological history

<Note>
  An agent-generated draft is not a sent message. Outreach is sent only after you review it and choose to send.
</Note>

## Reply detection

For outreach sent through the connected Gmail account, the Deal Sync routine checks for replies and updates the deal context:

- A positive reply can move the deal to Opportunity and trigger a new plan.
- A neutral reply updates the plan without forcing a stage change.
- A negative reply is recorded so the agent does not plan from stale assumptions.

Gmail and Calendar events can provide real-time context; the background check provides a catch-up path when needed.

## Proposals and invoices

The pipeline connects sales activity to the documents that close and collect the work.

- **Draft a proposal** from the deal so the client, scope context, and pipeline link stay together.
- **Convert an issued or approved proposal to an invoice** when you are ready to bill.
- **Link an invoice** so issued and paid status can update the deal's commercial context.
- **Move to Won** after linking or creating the client record required for the closed deal.

See [Invoices & Proposals](/billing/invoices-and-proposals) for the full proposal-to-invoice workflow.

## Human control and learning

Manual stage moves are authoritative: the agent replans around the stage you chose. Every also learns from durable signals such as rejections with reasons, edited outreach, stage moves, and won/lost outcomes. You can review Warm Lead preferences separately from the plan for an individual deal.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Every send outreach automatically?">
    No. The pipeline agent prepares the plan and draft, but you review the message and initiate the send. Unattended routines can prepare work but cannot send external messages on their own.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Pending a pipeline stage?">
    No. Pending is a review queue for agent-discovered matches. A lead enters the active pipeline only after approval.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does Every need a client before Won?">
    The client record connects the deal to proposals, invoices, payments, and revenue. If one is missing, Every asks you to link an existing client or create one from the deal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I move a deal myself?">
    Yes. Drag the deal to another stage. Your move takes precedence, and the agent updates the plan for the stage you selected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
