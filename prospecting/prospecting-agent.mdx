## Overview

Every's **Prospecting Agent** finds potential clients among people you already know. It searches the contacts and relationship data you choose to connect, researches promising matches, scores them for fit and relationship strength, and puts the best matches in **Pending** for review.

This is warm-network prospecting, not a cold-contact database. Every does not buy a lead list or send automatic outreach to everyone it finds.

## How warm-lead discovery works

When you run a search, Every:

1. Reads what your business sells and the audience you want to reach.
2. Excludes contacts that are already clients, already being worked, or have been rejected.
3. Enriches contacts that need more professional context before they can be evaluated.
4. Reviews relationship evidence and professional fit across the eligible network.
5. Researches the strongest candidates more deeply.
6. Saves qualified matches to **Pending** with a reason, evidence, and score.

The search keeps working toward the number of leads you requested. If it cannot reach that number, the result explains whether the remaining network was already represented, missing enough professional data, previously reviewed, or otherwise ineligible.

## Where Every looks

Every searches the relationship sources you have added to your workspace:

- Contacts you created or imported
- Google Contacts
- Gmail correspondence and Google Calendar meeting history, when those permissions are connected
- LinkedIn connections and message history you imported
- Contacts brought in through supported integrations or enrichment

Every uses evidence from the relationship itself rather than treating one source as inherently better. A first-degree LinkedIn connection, recent correspondence, meetings, and a complete professional record can all help establish that a relationship is warm.

<Note>
  Every only uses sources you connect or import. Google Drive access is file-by-file, and Google permissions can be managed separately. See [Google Workspace](/integrations/google-workspace).
</Note>

## How leads are scored

The Pending review shows the two primary decision scores:

- **Fit** — how closely the person matches what you sell and who you want to reach.
- **Relationship** — how strong the available path is based on your recorded history and connection evidence.

The agent also considers timing, confidence, professional context, and contactability when deciding whether to surface a match. Open a Pending lead to see the evidence and rationale behind the recommendation; a score is a decision aid, not proof that someone will buy.

## Choose who you want to find

Open **Deals → Find warm leads** to set the audience for a search.

### Scheduled search

The scheduled Warm Leads routine uses your saved targeting and searches automatically. You can manage its schedule and the number of new matches it should find from the same dialog.

Your saved targeting persists between runs. Updating it lets Every reconsider contacts against the new audience without wiping your existing review history.

### Custom search

A custom search is a one-time run with a specific target and lead count. It does not change the scheduled search. You can reuse a past target, or describe a new one in plain language.

Examples:

```text
Agency founders in Toronto who may need fractional product leadership
Past clients and collaborators who work at B2B software companies
Marketing leaders in my network who may need a brand refresh this quarter
```

Running the same target again continues through the eligible network instead of starting the search history over.

## Review before anything happens

Every puts every discovered match in **Pending**. At this point the lead has a rationale and score, but no outreach draft has been sent and the person is not yet in the active pipeline.

- **Approve** moves the match to **Lead**. The pipeline agent then builds the sales plan and prepares the first outreach draft.
- **Reject** removes the match and suppresses that contact from future searches. You can add a reason to teach Every what to avoid.

Nothing is sent from Pending. Later, when the pipeline prepares outreach, you still review the exact message before sending it.

## Teach Every what a good lead looks like

Every learns from the leads you approve or reject and from guidance you add under **Warm lead preferences**. Use preferences for durable rules such as:

```text
Prioritize founders who have raised a seed round in the last year.
Do not suggest direct competitors.
Favor people I have met or emailed in the last two years.
```

You can review and remove learned preferences. Rejecting a contact suppresses that person globally; the optional reason helps improve the broader scoring pattern.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this cold lead generation?">
    No. Every starts with the contacts and relationship sources you authorize. It can enrich and research those records, but it does not sell a third-party list of strangers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will Every message people automatically?">
    No. Matches first appear in Pending. You approve a lead before the pipeline prepares outreach, and you review the message before it is sent.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I reject a lead?">
    The contact is removed from review and suppressed from future searches, even if you later change your targeting. Adding a reason also gives the agent a useful preference signal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did a search find fewer leads than I requested?">
    The eligible network may be smaller than the target. Existing clients, active or previously reviewed deals, rejected contacts, and records without enough professional data can all be excluded. The completed run summarizes what happened.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
