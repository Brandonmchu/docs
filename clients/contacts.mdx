## Overview

Contacts are the **people** in your business world - everyone from active client contacts to potential leads you met at a conference. Unlike [Clients](/clients/clients) (which represent companies or businesses), Contacts represent individuals.

**Key capabilities:**
- Store contact details (name, email, phone, job title)
- Import from [LinkedIn](https://app.every.ai/contacts) and [Google Contacts](https://app.every.ai/contacts)
- Link contacts to one or more [clients](/clients/clients) with role-based email routing
- AI-powered enrichment and relationship summaries
- Google Contacts sync for duplicate detection

## Importing Contacts

Every supports multiple ways to bulk-import contacts into your network.

### LinkedIn Import

Import your LinkedIn connections directly from a CSV export, with optional AI relationship summaries from your message history.

**How to export from LinkedIn:**
1. Go to [LinkedIn Data Privacy settings](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/categories/privacy)
2. Click **"Get a copy of your data"**
3. Select **"Connections"** (and optionally **"Messages"** for AI summaries) and click **"Request archive"**
4. Wait for the email - LinkedIn often sends two exports over 24-48 hours. The second, larger one is the one you want
5. Download the ZIP and extract it - find **Connections.csv** (and optionally **Messages.csv**)

<Frame caption="On LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Download your data">
  <img src="/images/linkedin-download-data.png" alt="LinkedIn Data privacy settings with Download your data highlighted" />
</Frame>

**How to import:**
1. Go to [**Contacts**](https://app.every.ai/contacts) → **Import** dropdown → **LinkedIn**
2. Upload your `Connections.csv` file
3. Preview new contacts (duplicates are auto-filtered)
4. Click **Import**

**What gets imported:** Name, email (if available), job title, company (in notes), LinkedIn profile URL

<Accordion title="AI Relationship Summaries (Optional)">
  After importing contacts, upload your LinkedIn `Messages.csv` to generate AI-powered relationship timelines.

  1. After contact import completes, click **"Upload Messages.csv"**
  2. Every matches your message history to imported contacts via LinkedIn profile URLs
  3. AI analyzes your conversations and generates concise summaries
  4. Summaries appear in each contact's notes section

  **Example AI summary:**
  ```
  ## LinkedIn Message History
  2024 - Connected and discussed potential collaboration
  2024 - Caught up at industry conference
  2025 - Discussed hiring for board company
  ```

  Relationship summaries help Every Agent understand your history with each contact, enabling better personalized communication suggestions.
</Accordion>

### Google Contacts Import

Import your entire Google Contacts library with one click.

**How to import:**
1. Go to [**Contacts**](https://app.every.ai/contacts) → **Import** dropdown → **Google Contacts**
2. Authorize Google Contacts access if not already connected
3. Preview new vs. existing contacts
4. Click **Import**

**What gets imported:** Name, email, phone, organization, social URLs (LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.)

<Note>
  Google Contacts **Import** builds your contact pool. [Warm Leads](/prospecting/prospecting-agent) is the separate AI workflow that evaluates that pool for people who match what you sell.
</Note>

### Find warm leads from imported contacts

After importing contacts, open [**Pipeline**](https://app.every.ai/pipeline) → **Find warm leads**. Describe who you want to reach and how many matches you want. Every ranks eligible contacts using professional fit and available relationship evidence, then puts qualified matches in Pending for review.

Import does not turn every person into a client or deal. The prospecting review and your approval determine who enters the sales pipeline.

## Migrating from another CRM

Use a spreadsheet export when you are moving historical records from another CRM or invoicing tool.

1. Export the source data as CSV, XLSX, or XLS.
2. Open [**Data → Import**](https://app.every.ai/data/import) and upload one representative file.
3. Review the Import Agent's proposed mapping, especially contact-versus-client identity, dates, currencies, status values, and relationships between records.
4. Correct the mapping before the final import.
5. After completion, compare record counts and spot-check several contacts, clients, invoices, and balances.

The Import Agent can map connected business records such as clients, contacts, invoices, line items, payments, services, expenses, and time entries. Imports support undo receipts, but source exports are still your rollback reference—keep an untouched copy until you have verified the result.

<Note>
  Importing data into Every is different from connecting HubSpot or Xero for live agent access. Use import for records that should live in Every; use an integration when the external system remains the source of truth.
</Note>

## Linking Contacts to Clients

Contacts exist independently but can be **linked to one or more [clients](/clients/clients)** with role-based email routing. This means the same contact can be associated with multiple client companies.

### How Linking Works

When you link a contact to a client, you assign roles that control email routing:

**Invoice Role:**
- **Primary (TO):** Receives the main invoice email - exactly one required per client
- **CC:** Copied on invoice emails - can have multiple
- **None:** Not included in invoice emails

**Proposal Role:**
- **Primary (TO):** Receives the main proposal email
- **CC:** Copied on proposal emails
- **None:** Not included

**Example Setup:**
```
ACME Corp (Client) Contacts:
├── John Smith (CEO)
│   └── Invoices: CC | Proposals: Primary
├── Sarah Johnson (CFO)
│   └── Invoices: Primary | Proposals: CC
└── Accounts Team
    └── Invoices: CC | Proposals: None
```

### Linking from Client Page

1. Open a [client](https://app.every.ai/clients) → **Edit** → **Add Contact**
2. Search for an existing contact or create a new one
3. Set invoice and proposal roles
4. First contact defaults to Primary for both

### Linking via Agent

<Tip>
  **Ask Every Agent:** Manage contact-client links:
  - "Link John Smith to ACME Corp as the primary invoice contact"
  - "Add Sarah as a CC on proposals for TechStart Inc"
  - "Show me all contacts linked to ACME Corp"
  - "Set the billing team contact as CC on invoices for all enterprise clients"
</Tip>

<Note>
  You must have exactly one Primary (TO) contact per document type on each client. You cannot have CC recipients without a Primary.
</Note>

## Contact Enrichment

AI-powered enrichment helps fill in contact details automatically.

**During client enrichment:** When you [enrich a client](/clients/clients#client-enrichment), Every Agent can discover additional contacts at the company by scanning Gmail headers and Google Contacts for matching email domains.

**Google Contacts sync:** Every tracks which of your contacts exist in team members' Google Contacts. A sync indicator shows on matching contacts.

## Contacts vs. Clients

Understanding the distinction:

| | Contacts | Clients |
|-|-|-|
| **Represents** | People (individuals) | Companies/businesses |
| **Examples** | John Smith, Sarah Johnson | ACME Corp, TechStart Inc |
| **Relationship** | Can be linked to multiple clients | Has multiple contacts linked |
| **Primary use** | Email routing, relationship tracking | Invoicing, proposals, billing |
| **Import from** | LinkedIn, Google Contacts | Manual, Google scan, enrichment |

```
Contact (Person)                    Client (Company)
├── John Smith ──────────────────── ACME Corp
│   ├── email: john@acme.com        ├── Invoices
│   ├── title: CEO                   ├── Proposals
│   └── roles:                       ├── Time Tracking
│       ├── ACME: Invoice CC         ├── Expenses
│       └── ACME: Proposal Primary   └── Automations
│
├── Sarah Johnson ───────────────── ACME Corp
│   └── roles:                       (same client, different roles)
│       └── ACME: Invoice Primary
│
└── Mike Chen ───────────────────── TechStart Inc
    └── roles:                       (different client entirely)
        └── TechStart: Invoice Primary
```

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a contact and a client?">
    A contact is a person. A client is the business record used for proposals, invoices, expenses, and time. One contact can be linked to more than one client with separate invoice and proposal recipient roles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does importing contacts put everyone into my sales pipeline?">
    No. Import creates contact records. Warm Leads separately evaluates eligible contacts, and each match stays in Pending until you approve it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I migrate historical CRM data, not just contacts?">
    Yes. Use Data → Import with a CSV or Excel export. The Import Agent can map multiple related Every record types; review its proposed relationships and status mapping before the write.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does Every prevent duplicate contacts?">
    Direct imports use email and source identity to skip or match obvious duplicates. No automatic rule can resolve every messy CRM export, so review the preview and spot-check people who share names, emails, or companies.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

**Contact not receiving invoices:** Verify the contact is linked to the client with a Primary or CC invoice role. Check email address is correct.

**Duplicate contacts after import:** Open **Review duplicates** on the Contacts page. Every groups likely matches by confidence; review uncertain pairs before merging. Recent merges can be undone from that workflow.

**Can't delete contact:** Contact may be set as Primary recipient on a client. Set another contact as Primary first.

**LinkedIn import missing emails:** Many LinkedIn users don't share email addresses. Contacts are still imported by name - enrich later to find contact details.
