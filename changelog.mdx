## July 2026

### Safer approvals from email and connected AI tools

Released July 31, 2026.

Sensitive actions requested outside the Every app now have durable, single-use approval flows.

**What's new:**
- **Email invoice deletion** — ask `agent@every.ai` naturally, then approve or cancel by replying in the same thread with the exact human-readable command Every provides
- **MCP approval cards** — review the complete action details in Every before ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, the Every CLI, or another Admin MCP client performs a higher-impact action
- **Timeout-safe retries** — if an MCP client stops waiting, approve in Every and retry the identical request within 10 minutes
- **Replay protection** — approvals are bound to the exact action and can be consumed only once

**How to use it:**
See the [approval guide](/reference/approvals), [connect an AI tool](/integrations/connect-ai-tools), or email [`agent@every.ai`](mailto:agent@every.ai).

### Every AI for Slack — Early Access

Released July 26, 2026 (Early Access).

Talk to Every without leaving Slack. Connect your workspace once, and your team can DM the agent privately or @mention it in channels — same agent, same data, same permissions as the app.

**What's new:**
- **Workspace connection** — an admin links Slack to your Every organization from [Agent Settings → Connections](https://every.ai/agent/connections)
- **Per-person identity** — each teammate links their own Slack identity to their own Every account; answers always run as the person asking
- **Private DMs and public channel threads** — DMs are a continuous private conversation; channel @mentions get threaded answers everyone can read
- **Rich record cards** — invoices and other records come back as cards with an **Open in Every** button
- **In-Slack approvals** — consequential actions (like sending an invoice) show Approve/Deny buttons that only the asker can click
- **Attachments** — paste a screenshot or attach a document (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and more) and the agent reads it, just like in-app chat

**How to use it:**
Early Access is open to pilot customers — contact [hello@every.ai](mailto:hello@every.ai). Full guide: [Every AI for Slack](/integrations/slack).

## June 2026

### Heartbeat — Scheduled Agent Routines

Released June 2, 2026.

The agent's **Automations** hub is now **Heartbeat** — the home for recurring work your agent runs on a schedule. Routines are grouped into Briefings, Pipeline, Prospecting, and Other, so you can see and manage everything the agent does in the background from one place.

**What's new:**
- **Renamed hub** — Automations is now **Heartbeat** at `/agent/heartbeat`, with routines grouped by area
- **Pipeline Review** — a scheduled scan that re-checks active deals and re-plans only the ones that changed, attributed to each deal's owner
- **Prospecting Scan** — schedule the prospecting agent to mine your network for new opportunities on a recurring cadence
- **Weekly Business Review** — a scheduled revenue, pipeline, and operations digest with recommended actions
- **Per-routine scheduling** — set the cadence (e.g. every Monday at 8:00 AM) right from the dashboard pills

**How to use it:**
[Agent Settings → Heartbeat](https://every.ai/agent/heartbeat) → Enable a built-in routine or create a custom scheduled prompt → Set its schedule

<Tip>
  **Ask Every Agent:** "Set up a daily prospecting scan" or "Schedule a weekly business review every Monday morning"
</Tip>

### Prospecting & Pipeline

Released June 2, 2026.

Every now helps you **find clients and win the work**: an AI prospecting agent that surfaces new opportunities, and a sales pipeline to move approved deals from lead to won.

**What's new:**
- **AI Prospecting Agent** — scans your warm network, scores each contact on fit, relationship, and timing, and drafts outreach for the most promising leads
- **Pending review queue** — every suggested deal lands in a review column for you to approve or dismiss; nothing is sent without your go-ahead
- **Sales pipeline** — approved deals move across a Lead → Opportunity → Won/Lost kanban with agent recommendations and drag-to-stage
- **Per-deal replan & deal sync** — the agent re-plans individual deals and scans Gmail for replies to keep stages current
- **Auto-move to Won & proposal integration** — closed deals advance automatically, and you can send proposals straight from a deal

**How to use it:**
Open [**Pipeline**](https://every.ai/pipeline) → run a scan or review the Pending queue → approve deals to move them onto the board

<Tip>
  **Ask Every Agent:** "Find me new prospects from my network" or "What's the status of my pipeline?"
</Tip>

### Daily Brief — Your AI Chief of Staff

Released June 3, 2026.

The old one-line "Daily Inbox Summary" is now a full **Daily Brief** — a managed-agent morning digest that cross-references your email, calendar, invoices, accounts receivable, and pipeline into a single briefing.

**What's new:**
- **Narrative + "Your Move" + Snapshot** — a written summary of what matters today, your highest-leverage next actions, and a metrics snapshot
- **"Since your last brief, your agents…"** — synthesizes what your other Heartbeat routines (prospecting, pipeline reviews) did since the last brief
- **Per-user and private** — in a multi-user org, each member gets their own private brief

**How to use it:**
[Agent Settings → Heartbeat](https://every.ai/agent/heartbeat) → enable **Daily Brief** and set when it runs

### Teams — Multi-User Organizations

Released June 8, 2026.

Every now works as a genuine multi-user workspace, so a team can run a client business together without stepping on each other.

**What's new:**
- **Deal ownership & attribution** — each pipeline deal has an owner; outreach is sent from and reply-scanned against the owner's mailbox
- **Thread privacy** — chats can be private to you or shared with the org; threads that touch your personal Google data default to private
- **Per-member identity** — avatars, owner labels, and an All / My filter across the workspace
- **Per-user Google scoping** — each member connects their own Google account, scoped to them

**How to use it:**
Invite teammates in **Organization Settings → Team**, then assign deal owners on the [Pipeline](https://every.ai/pipeline).

### Services Bulk Delete — Smart Split

Released June 4, 2026.

Bulk-deleting services no longer fails when some are still in use. Every now splits the action automatically.

**What's new:**
- **Smart split** — unused services are deleted, while services referenced by invoices, recurring invoices, time tracking, or bookings are **archived** instead (preserving history)
- **Clear feedback** — a toast reports exactly what was deleted vs. archived

**How to use it:**
[**Services**](https://every.ai/services) → select multiple → **Delete**. Archived services move to **Services → Archived**.

### Voice & Tone

Released March 25, 2026.

Every Agent now has a dedicated **Voice & Tone** workspace that helps it write more consistently on behalf of your business.

**What's new:**
- **Audience-specific voice profiles** — separate guidance for client-facing, internal/casual, and non-client external writing
- **Generated from email history** — Every can build an initial voice profile from how your business already communicates

  **Ask Every Agent:** "Draft this proposal in my normal client-facing tone" or "Write a follow-up note that sounds like us"

### Branded Client Portal & Public URLs

Released March 10, 2026.

Every now supports a branded client-facing surface across your portal, invoices, proposals, and booking flow.

**What's new:**
- **Branded subdomain** — use `{your-slug}.every.ai` for client-facing links
- **Client portal** — contacts can log in with a magic link to view their invoices and proposals
- **Shared public surface** — invoices, proposals, and booking can all live under the same branded domain
- **Portal shortcuts** — direct links for invoice and proposal sections on the portal
- **Backward compatibility** — root-domain public links still work, while branded links become the preferred experience

**How to use it:**
Set your subdomain slug in organization settings, then share branded links like `{your-slug}.every.ai`, `{your-slug}.every.ai/invoice/{public_id}`, `{your-slug}.every.ai/proposal/{public_id}`, and `{your-slug}.every.ai/book`

<Tip>
  Use the client portal when you want clients to revisit invoices and proposals without digging through old email threads.
</Tip>

### Client Booking Page

Released March 5, 2026.

Let clients book appointments directly through your public booking page at `{your-slug}.every.ai/book`.

**What's new:**
- **Public booking page** — clients select a service, pick a date and time, fill in details, and pay if required
- **3 booking modes** — Standard (date + time + payment), Free (no payment), and Schedule Later (you coordinate timing after)
- **Flexible payment collection** — no payment, pay at booking via Stripe Checkout, or save card and charge before service
- **Weekly availability + overrides** — set recurring hours and block off specific dates
- **Google Calendar integration** — block busy slots automatically and create calendar events on booking
- **Automated emails** — confirmation, 24-hour reminder, 1-hour reminder, cancellation, and reschedule notifications
- **Client self-service** — clients cancel or reschedule via a secure link (no login needed)
- **Deep data integration** — uses your existing Services, auto-creates Contacts and Clients, auto-creates paid Invoices
- **Booking policy editor** — add cancellation terms and instructions with rich text formatting

**How to use it:**
[Booking Settings](https://every.ai/clients/bookings/settings) → Select bookable services → Set availability → Share your link

<Tip>
  **Ask Every Agent:** "Create a booking for Sarah Chen next Tuesday at 2pm" or "Show me all upcoming bookings this week"
</Tip>

### Client Contacts System
