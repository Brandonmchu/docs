## Overview

Every AI for Slack puts the same agent you chat with in the app directly into your Slack workspace. DM it for a private conversation, or @mention it in a channel so your whole team can see the answer.

**What You Can Do:**
- Ask anything you'd ask in the Every app — invoices, clients, deals, expenses, reports
- DM **Every AI** for a private, continuous conversation (like your in-app chat)
- @mention **@Every AI** in any channel it's been invited to — it answers in a thread, visible to everyone there
- Paste a screenshot or attach a file (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and more) — the agent reads it, same as in-app chat
- Get rich cards for things like invoices, with an **Open in Every** button that jumps straight to the record
- Approve or deny consequential actions (like sending an invoice) with buttons right in Slack

## How It Works

One Slack workspace connects to one Every organization. Each person then links their own Slack identity to their own Every account — so when you ask a question, the agent answers **as you**, and can only see what you could already see in Every.

That means two teammates asking the same question can get different answers: each person sees their own data, with their own permissions, exactly like in the app.

## Connecting Your Workspace (Admin)

An organization admin connects Slack once for the whole team:

Go to [**Agent → Connections**](https://app.every.ai/agent/connections) → find the **Slack** card → **Connect your Slack workspace** → review the Slack consent screen → **Allow**.

That's it — the workspace is now linked to your Every organization. You can disconnect at any time from the same card, which immediately stops the bot for everyone.

<Note>
  Connecting the workspace doesn't give anyone access by itself. Each teammate still links their own account (below), and only members of your Every organization can talk to the bot.
</Note>

## Linking Your Account (Everyone)

The first time you DM **Every AI** or @mention it in a channel, it replies with a one-time sign-in link:

1. Click the link — it opens Every and asks you to sign in (if you aren't already)
2. Your Slack identity is now bound to **your** Every account
3. Go back to Slack and ask your question again — from now on, every answer runs as you

You only do this once. If you're in the Slack workspace but **not** a member of the Every organization, the sign-in link won't complete — ask your admin to invite you (see [Team Access](#team-access-who-can-use-it) below).

## Using It Day to Day

### Direct Messages — private, continuous

A DM with Every AI works like your in-app chat: one continuous private conversation that remembers context. Ask follow-ups naturally — "what about last month?" works because it remembers what you were just talking about.

**Use it for:** anything you'd rather keep private — revenue questions, drafting a sensitive email, checking overdue invoices.

### Channels — public, threaded

@mention **@Every AI** in any channel it's been invited to (`/invite @Every AI`). It answers in a thread on your message, and everyone in the channel can read it. Each thread is its own conversation.

**Use it for:** questions the whole team should see — "@Every AI what's our pipeline looking like this week?" in your sales channel.

<Tip>
  Rule of thumb: ask **privately in a DM**, ask **publicly in a channel**. That's by design — channel answers are team knowledge; DM answers are yours.
</Tip>

### Rich cards and links

When the agent references a record like an invoice, it posts a card with the key details and an **Open in Every** button that takes you straight to that record in the app.

### Approvals

Consequential actions — like actually sending an invoice to a client — don't just run. The agent posts **Approve / Deny** buttons in the thread first. Only the person who asked can click them; the agent proceeds only on Approve.

## What the Bot Can (and Can't) See

Every AI for Slack is deliberately narrow about what it reads:

- **It only sees messages addressed to it** — your DMs with it, and messages that @mention it
- **It does not read channel history.** Inviting it to a channel doesn't let it see the conversation — only the messages that mention it
- **Attachments work like in-app chat** — screenshots and images are read visually; documents (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and more) are processed as files. Up to 10 files per message, 20MB each. It only ever reads files in messages addressed to it — never your workspace's other files

## Team Access — Who Can Use It

Access to the bot follows your **Every organization membership**, not your Slack membership:

- Anyone in the Slack workspace can **see** the bot and **read** its channel answers
- Only members of your Every organization can **ask** it anything

So if a channel has ten people and five of them have Every accounts in your organization, those five can talk to the agent — the other five can read along but will hit a sign-in prompt if they try to ask.

**To give a teammate access:** invite them to your Every organization first. Go to **Settings → Business**, open the **Team** section, and invite them by email. Once they've joined, they link their Slack identity like everyone else and they're in.

<Note>
  There's no separate "enable for everyone" switch in Slack — a Slack admin can add the bot to every channel, but each person who wants to *use* it needs a seat in your Every organization. Everyone in the channel can read; only Every members can ask.
</Note>

## Disconnecting

**To Disconnect:** Agent → Connections → Slack → Disconnect.

This immediately stops the bot for the whole workspace. Your Every data is untouched, and you can reconnect anytime.
