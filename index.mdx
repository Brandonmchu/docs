## What is Every?

Every is the **AI-native platform for client businesses** — the place to **find clients, win the work, and get paid**, with an AI agent that runs the day-to-day for you.

It pairs everything you need to operate a services or product business — clients, proposals, invoices, payments, bookings — with an autonomous agent that prospects for new business, keeps your pipeline moving, and handles recurring busywork so you can focus on the client.

The platform spans the full arc of client work:

- **Find clients** — the AI **Prospecting Agent** mines your warm network, scores fit, and drafts outreach, surfacing new opportunities into a review queue
- **Win the work** — track deals on an AI-driven **Pipeline**, send branded proposals, and let clients book time directly
- **Get paid** — create professional invoices, accept online payments via Stripe, automate recurring billing, and chase outstanding balances
- **Run it on autopilot** — **Every Agent** handles requests in chat, while **Heartbeat** runs scheduled routines (daily briefs, pipeline reviews, prospecting scans) in the background
