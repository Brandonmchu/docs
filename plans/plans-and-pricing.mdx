## How Every's plans work

Every is **feature-uniform across plans**. There is no "Pro-only" invoicing feature, no "Core-only" agent, no locked integrations. Invoices, proposals, payments, recurring billing, expenses, bookings, the client portal, Every Agent, Scheduled Tasks, Prospecting & Pipeline, and every integration are available on every plan.

The real difference between plans is **how much AI you can use each month** — measured in [credits](/plans/what-is-a-credit). Plus, one feature (the **Referral program**) is available only on paid plans.

## The plans

| Plan | Monthly AI credits | Referral program | Everything else |
|-|-|-|-|
| **Free** | 14-day trial, 1,000 credits, no credit card required | Not available | All features |
| **Every Core** | 10,000 credits / month | Available | All features |
| **Every Pro** | 50,000 credits / month | Available | All features |

<Note>
  "Credits" here are **display credits** — the unit you see in **Settings &gt; Billing**. Every Pro's 50,000/month is 5× Every Core's 10,000/month. See [What is a credit?](/plans/what-is-a-credit) for what a credit represents and what consumes them.
</Note>

### Free

The Free plan is your **14-day free trial** — no credit card required. It includes **1,000 AI credits** and the entire product — agent, invoicing, prospecting, everything — the same on every platform (web, mobile, or an MCP/AI-tool connector signup).

A few things to know about the trial:

- **No credit card required to start.** Sign up and start using Every right away.
- The trial ends after **14 days**, or sooner if you use all **1,000 credits** — whichever comes first. Once it ends, AI features pause and the app routes you to billing; some non-AI actions (sending an invoice, downloading a PDF) are paused too, until you add credits or subscribe. See [Credit usage & errors](/plans/credit-usage-and-errors).
- **Anything you buy keeps working.** Extra credits purchased during the trial never expire and carry straight over once you subscribe.
- The Referral program is not available during the trial.

See [the free trial section](/plans/credit-usage-and-errors#how-big-is-the-free-trial) for the full details.

### Every Core

Every Core is the entry paid plan. It includes **10,000 credits per month** that reset at the start of each billing period, plus the full feature set and the [Referral program](#referral-program-paid-plans-only).

Unused monthly credits do **not** roll over. If you need more in a given month, you can buy [extra credits](/plans/what-is-a-credit#extra-credits) (these never expire and are used after your monthly allocation runs out) or upgrade to Every Pro.

### Every Pro

Every Pro includes **50,000 credits per month** — 5× the Every Core allocation — with the same full feature set and Referral program. It's the right plan if you lean heavily on Scheduled Tasks, prospecting, the daily brief, and frequent agent work.

As with Core, unused monthly credits don't roll over, and you can top up with extra credits anytime.

## Referral program (paid plans only)

The Referral program ("Refer &amp; Earn") is the one feature that is **paid-only** — it's available on Every Core and Every Pro, but not on Free. Paid users get a share link and can invite others; eligible referrals grant credits to both sides. You'll find it in **Settings &gt; Refer &amp; earn** (hidden on Free orgs).

## Choosing a plan

Because features are identical across plans, the question is simply **how much AI you'll use per month**:

- **Trying Every?** Start your **14-day free trial** — no credit card required.
- **Steady day-to-day agent + invoicing use?** **Every Core** (10,000 credits/month) is the common choice.
- **Heavy automation** — daily briefs, prospecting scans, pipeline reviews, lots of agent chat? **Every Pro** (50,000 credits/month) gives you 5× the headroom.

<Tip>
  Not sure how many credits you'll use? Subscribe to **Every Core**, watch the Monthly Credits usage bar in **Settings &gt; Billing** for a cycle, and upgrade to **Every Pro** if you're consistently running low. You can also enable [auto-recharge](/plans/what-is-a-credit#auto-recharge) so background tasks never get interrupted.
</Tip>
