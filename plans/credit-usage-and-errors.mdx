This page answers the questions people most often have about how credits are charged — especially around errors, running out, and scheduled tasks. For what a credit is and what consumes them, see [What is a credit?](/plans/what-is-a-credit); for plan amounts, see [Plans &amp; Pricing](/plans/plans-and-pricing).

## Charges & failures

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Am I charged if an AI task fails?">
    **You're charged for the work the model actually did, even if the run fails partway through.** Credits meter the AI work that happened — so if a task got partway and then errored, the work up to that point still consumes credits. There is **no automatic refund or rollback**.

    The one exception: a run that fails **immediately, with zero AI work done**, is **not** charged. If the model never did any work, you aren't billed for it.

    So in short:

    - Run did some work, then errored → charged for the work done, no refund.
    - Run failed instantly with no model usage → not charged.

    If you believe you were charged in error, contact [hello@every.ai](mailto:hello@every.ai).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will a failed automation retry?">
    Background and scheduled jobs automatically retry transient infrastructure errors (and Every avoids double-billing across those retries). They do **not** retry when the failure is due to running out of credits or a logical error in the task — for those, check the activity timeline and the linked thread, fix the cause, and run it again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Running out of credits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    It depends on your plan.

    **Trial workspaces hit a full gate.** When your 14-day trial ends — either the 14 days are up, or your 10,000 trial credits run out, whichever comes first — the app routes you to plan selection. Your workspace and data remain saved, but an admin must choose a plan before normal work resumes.

    **Paid workspaces hit an AI-only limit, not a lockout.** When a Solo or Team workspace uses up its credits, AI features pause with an upgrade, top-up, or reset prompt, but the rest of the app keeps working normally — you can still send invoices, download PDFs, record payments, and use non-AI features. There is no full-app redirect.

    To restore AI access in either case:

    - **Choose Solo or Team** when a trial ends or its credits are exhausted.
    - **Buy [extra credits](/plans/what-is-a-credit#extra-credits)** on a paid plan for immediate access (they are used after the monthly allocation).
    - **Upgrade from Solo to Team** for a larger monthly allocation.
    - **Wait for your monthly reset** if you are already on a paid plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I get warned before I run out?">
    Every does not currently promise a pre-exhaustion email. When a paid workspace uses up its monthly credits, Every sends a notification email shortly after it reaches zero (skipped if you have already topped up or upgraded in that window). To reduce interruptions — especially if you rely on scheduled tasks — enable [auto-recharge](/plans/what-is-a-credit#auto-recharge), which buys extra credits automatically when your balance drops below a threshold you set.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Scheduled tasks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My scheduled task says Completed but nothing happened.">
    If your credits were **exhausted** when a scheduled or background task was due, Every **skips** the run and marks it **Completed** — a no-op. The task didn't fail; there were simply no credits to do the work, so nothing ran. You won't get a separate per-skip email, push, or in-app notice for each skipped run.

    What to do:

    1. Top up with [extra credits](/plans/what-is-a-credit#extra-credits), or wait for your monthly reset (paid plans).
    2. Re-run the task with **Run Now** if you need it immediately.

    To prevent silent skips on tasks you depend on, enable [auto-recharge](/plans/what-is-a-credit#auto-recharge) so a low balance never interrupts your automations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know if a scheduled task actually failed?">
    A task that **ran out of credits** is marked Completed (see above) — that's a skip, not a failure. A task that genuinely **errored** while running shows up in the activity timeline and the linked thread. Mobile users with notifications enabled may also get a failure push; there isn't a proactive web notification for every failure, so the activity timeline is the place to check.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Free trial & monthly resets

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How big is the free trial?">
    Every new workspace gets a **14-day free trial** — no credit card required — with **10,000 AI credits** included. This is the same on every platform: web, mobile, and MCP/AI-tool connector signups all start with the same trial clock and base grant.

    The trial ends after **14 days** or when the **10,000 credits** run out, whichever comes first. Trial credits live in your extra-credits bucket and do **not** reset. (Some promotions, like the [Google-connection bonus](/integrations/connect-ai-tools), can add credits on top of the base grant.)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do my credits reset?">
    **Paid plans** include a monthly credit allocation (Solo: 10,000; Team: 50,000) that **resets at the start of each billing period**. The reset takes effect the first time you use Every after the new period begins.

    A few details:

    - **Unused monthly credits do not roll over** — each period starts fresh at your plan's allocation.
    - **Extra credits never expire** and are spent only after your monthly allocation runs out.
    - **Trial orgs have no monthly reset** — the trial grant is one-time and doesn't refill, and the trial window itself closes 14 days after signup even if credits remain.

    Your exact reset date is shown on the Monthly Credits card in **Settings &gt; Billing**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
