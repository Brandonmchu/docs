## Overview

Every Agent is Every's conversational assistant. Instead of clicking through menus, you describe what you need in plain language and the agent creates, updates, analyzes, and drafts across your workspace.

It is not a chatbot bolted onto the side of the app — it operates the same data you do, through a large and continually growing toolset, with the same permissions and the same records.

**What makes it useful:**
- It works directly with your live business data in Every
- It uses your saved [Business Context and Voice & Tone](/ai/context-and-voice) so its work sounds like you
- It [remembers facts and applies skills](/ai/memory-and-skills) you've taught it across every chat
- It connects to external systems like Google Workspace, Xero, HubSpot, and Granola
- It handles multi-step workflows without you mapping every click

## What the Agent Can Reach

Every Agent works directly with your data in Every. It can read, create, update, and (with confirmation) delete across:

| Area | What the agent can do |
|-|-|
| **Clients** | Create and update clients, enrich company records, analyze concentration and revenue |
| **Contacts** | Create and link contacts; import from Google, LinkedIn, or CSV |
| **Invoices** | Draft, edit, apply tax and discounts, schedule sends, send to clients |
| **Proposals** | Draft structured proposals, track approval, convert approved proposals into invoices |
| **Payments** | Record manual payments, send reminders and confirmations, check Stripe-linked state |
| **Expenses** | Parse receipt uploads into expenses, add unbilled expenses to invoices |
| **Time** | Log time entries, add unbilled time to invoices |
| **Bookings** | Check upcoming bookings, create booking records |
| **Recurring invoices** | Create, pause, and update recurring invoice automations; review run history |

It also runs reporting and analysis on top of all of this — revenue summaries, overdue and cash-flow reviews, client concentration, and service or product trends.

The agent's toolset is large and grows continuously as new capabilities ship, so think of it less as a fixed menu and more as: *if you can do it in Every, you can usually ask the agent to do it.*

<Tip>
  **Ask Every Agent:**
  - "Create an invoice for ACME Corp for $5,000 for consulting services"
  - "Show me all overdue invoices and draft reminders"
  - "Find the HubSpot deal for Acme and summarize what stage it's in"
  - "Pull the action items from my Granola meeting with Sarah last week"
</Tip>

## How Every Agent Works

Every Agent operates in **direct execution mode**: you describe the task, the agent figures out the steps, performs them, and shows or confirms the result. You keep refining conversationally.

```text
You: "Create an invoice for ACME Corp for $5,000"
Agent: [Shows draft]
You: "Actually make it $5,500 and add a thank-you note"
Agent: [Updates the draft]
You: "Perfect, send it"
```

### Confirm Before It Sends or Deletes

The agent acts on your data, so the actions that **leave your workspace or remove data are gated**. The agent shows you what it's about to do and waits for your go-ahead before:

- **Sending** anything to a client — invoices, proposals, payment reminders, confirmations, and emails
- **Deleting** records — clients, contacts, invoices, proposals, payments, services, and more

Reads, drafts, and internal edits happen directly so the conversation stays fast; the irreversible, client-facing steps are the ones that ask first.

<Warning>
  Always double-check invoice and email details before confirming a send. Once an email is delivered to a client, it cannot be unsent.
</Warning>

## Navigation

You don't have to know where a page lives. Ask the agent to take you somewhere and it can navigate the app for you — "open the payments page," "take me to my overdue invoices," "show me ACME's client record." This pairs well with analysis: ask a question, then jump straight to the records behind the answer.

## Voice Input

Every Agent supports voice input. Click the microphone icon in the chat input, speak your request, and your speech is transcribed and sent as a normal message.

**Useful for:**
- Logging time while working
- Capturing expenses quickly
- Sending short requests from mobile

### Learned Preferences

Beyond freeform memory, the agent can save specific, standing business rules.

**Examples:**
- "ACME Corp always gets Net 15 terms"
- "Never apply tax to consulting services"
- "Enterprise clients default to 45-day terms"
- "Always include project reference numbers on TechStart invoices"

When the agent proposes saving a rule, it asks you to confirm. Manage saved rules in [**Agent → Preferences**](https://every.ai/agent/preferences).

<Note>
  Learned Preferences are standing rules ("always do X"). [Agent Memory](/ai/memory-and-skills) is freeform recall (running lists, observations, open todos). [Business Context](/ai/context-and-voice) is the narrative of what your business is. They work together.
</Note>

## Batch & Multi-Step Workflows

The agent can apply changes across many records at once, and break a goal into ordered steps.

```text
"Update all enterprise clients to Net 45"
"Archive clients with no invoice activity in the last 2 years"
"Change tax rate to 13% on all consulting services"
```

```text
"Set up my consulting business for 2026:
1. Create a new service for strategy work at $175/hour
2. Update my recurring automations to use the new rate
3. Draft a rate change note for retainer clients"
```

## Undoing Agent Actions

Every Agent includes a built-in undo system. When the agent creates or modifies data, an **Undo** button appears below its message so you can reverse the action if something is wrong.

### How It Works

- A yellow **Undo** button appears inline after any data-modifying action
- Hover to preview which items will be affected (they highlight in yellow)
- Click to reverse **all actions from that agent turn** (it's all-or-nothing to prevent inconsistent states)
- You can also undo from the **Activity Timeline** on the Home dashboard

### What Can Be Undone

Most data operations can be reversed:

- **Invoices & proposals** — creation, updates, status changes, line item additions
- **Payments & time tracking** — payment records, time entries, expense records
- **Clients & contacts** — creation, updates, contact linking
- **Complex operations** — multi-step workflows, bulk operations, CSV import results

### What Cannot Be Undone

- **Emails already sent** to clients (invoices, notifications)
- **Third-party actions** already processed (Stripe payments, Google Calendar events, HubSpot syncs)
- **Manually edited data** — if you edited the record after the agent action, undo is blocked to protect your changes

### Limitations

- Only the **most recent agent turn** can be undone — once the agent performs a new action, the previous undo buffer is replaced
- After undoing, you cannot "redo" — ask the agent again instead
- If a conflict is detected (manual edits, external processing), the system blocks undo and shows an explanation

<Tip>
  If you notice an error, undo immediately before the agent performs more actions. It is easier to undo right away than after additional turns.
</Tip>

## Activity History

Every action the agent takes is recorded in the **Activity Timeline** on your Home dashboard. Use it to review what the agent did, see which records changed, and undo a past turn even after you've moved on in the conversation.
