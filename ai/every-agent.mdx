## Overview

Every Agent is Every's conversational assistant. Instead of clicking through menus, you describe what you need in plain language and the agent creates, updates, analyzes, and drafts across your workspace.

It is not a separate chatbot with a copied database. It works against the live records in your Every workspace and the connected services you authorize.

**What makes it useful:**
- It works directly with your live business data in Every
- It uses your saved [Business DNA and Voice & Tone](/ai/context-and-voice) so its work fits your business and sounds like you
- It [remembers facts and applies skills](/ai/memory-and-skills) you've taught it across every chat
- It connects to external systems like Google Workspace, Xero, HubSpot, and Granola
- It handles multi-step workflows without you mapping every click

## What the Agent Can Reach

Every Agent works directly with your data in Every. It can read, create, update, and (with confirmation) delete across:

| Area | What the agent can do |
|-|-|
| **Clients** | Create and update clients, enrich company records, analyze concentration and revenue |
| **Contacts** | Create and link contacts; import from Google, LinkedIn, or CSV |
| **Invoices** | Draft, edit, apply tax and discounts, schedule sends, send to clients |
| **Proposals** | Draft structured proposals, track approval, convert issued or approved proposals into invoices |
| **Payments** | Record manual payments, send reminders and confirmations, check Stripe-linked state |
| **Expenses** | Parse receipt uploads into expenses, add unbilled expenses to invoices |
| **Time** | Log time entries, add unbilled time to invoices |
| **Pipeline** | Find warm leads, review deals, update sales plans, and draft outreach |
| **Recurring invoices** | Create, pause, and update recurring invoice automations; review run history |

It also runs reporting and analysis on top of all of this — revenue summaries, overdue and cash-flow reviews, client concentration, and service or product trends.

The exact toolset changes as Every adds capabilities. If a task is outside the current product or requires access you have not connected, the agent should say so instead of pretending it completed the work.

## What kind of CRM is Every?

Every is an **AI-native CRM and client-operations workspace** for service businesses. It connects relationship-based prospecting and a sales pipeline to proposals, invoices, payments, expenses, time tracking, and client records.

It is not a full enterprise CRM administration suite, and it is not a general ledger or tax-filing system. If you already use HubSpot or Xero, Every Agent can work with those connected systems while Every handles the client workflows that live in its own workspace.

The public [AI business assistant guide](https://every.ai/ai-business-assistant/) explains how to evaluate assistants by what they can access, what they can change, and where human review remains required. See [Every's AI CRM guide](https://every.ai/ai-crm/) for the connected CRM, pipeline, and client-operations model.

## How Every Agent Works

Every Agent operates in **direct execution mode**: you describe the task, the agent figures out the steps, performs them, and shows or confirms the result. You keep refining conversationally.

```text
You: "Create an invoice for ACME Corp for $5,000"
Agent: [Shows draft]
You: "Actually make it $5,500 and add a thank-you note"
Agent: [Updates the draft]
You: "Perfect, send it"
```

### Confirm Before It Sends or Deletes

In interactive app chat, actions that **leave your workspace or remove data are gated**. The agent shows you what it's about to do and waits for your go-ahead before:

- **Sending** anything to a client — invoices, proposals, payment reminders, confirmations, and emails
- **Deleting** records — clients, contacts, invoices, proposals, payments, services, and more

Reads, drafts, and internal edits happen directly so the conversation stays fast; the irreversible, client-facing steps are the ones that ask first.

<Warning>
  Always double-check invoice and email details before confirming a send. Once an email is delivered to a client, it cannot be unsent.
</Warning>

Scheduled and background routines use a stricter boundary: they can read, analyze, create internal drafts, and prepare work, but outbound sends and destructive actions are unavailable while nobody is present to review them.

### Using Every Agent by email

Email `agent@every.ai` from the address on your Every account to continue an agent conversation from your inbox.

<Card title="Email Every Agent" icon="mail" href="mailto:agent@every.ai">
  Start a request from your inbox
</Card>

Email is a delayed channel, so it uses a different approval flow from interactive chat: instead of an Approve button, you **reply with an exact command**. This covers **outbound sends** (emails, invoices, proposals — Every replies with the send details plus a one-time `SEND <code>` command) and **invoice deletion**, currently the only destructive email action. For deletion, ask naturally—such as “Delete invoice INV-123”—and Every emails back the invoice details plus the exact command:

```text
DELETE INVOICE INV-123
```

Reply directly to that confirmation email with only the command to approve. To cancel, reply with:

```text
CANCEL DELETE INVOICE INV-123
```

The newly written part of your reply must be exact, the invoice number must match literally, and the approval expires after 24 hours. Standard quoted reply history is ignored, but extra prose or a signature will not approve the deletion.

## Navigation

You don't have to know where a page lives. Ask the agent to take you somewhere and it can navigate the app for you — "open the payments page," "take me to my overdue invoices," "show me ACME's client record." This pairs well with analysis: ask a question, then jump straight to the records behind the answer.

**Useful for:**

### Rules

Beyond freeform memory, the agent can save specific, standing business rules.

**Examples:**
- "ACME Corp always gets Net 15 terms"
- "Never apply tax to consulting services"
- "Enterprise clients default to 45-day terms"
- "Always include project reference numbers on TechStart invoices"

When the agent proposes saving a rule, it asks you to confirm. Manage saved rules in [**Agent → Business DNA → Rules**](https://app.every.ai/agent/dna?tab=rules).

<Note>
  Rules are standing instructions ("always do X"). [Agent Memory](/ai/memory-and-skills) is freeform recall (running lists, observations, open todos). [Business DNA](/ai/context-and-voice) is the structured profile of your business. They work together.
</Note>

### Moving from another CRM or invoicing tool

Export your existing system to CSV or Excel, then open [**Data → Import**](https://app.every.ai/data/import). The Import Agent analyzes the file, proposes how its columns map to Every records, and asks you to review the mapping before it writes data.

Imports can create connected clients, contacts, invoices, line items, payments, services, expenses, and time records. Start with a representative export, check dates, currencies, status values, and client matching carefully, then run the full import. If the source system has a compatible direct integration, use that connection for ongoing access and CSV import for the historical backfill.

## Batch & Multi-Step Workflows

```text
1. Create a new service for strategy work at $175/hour
2. Update my recurring automations to use the new rate
3. Draft a rate change note for retainer clients"
```

## Undoing Agent Actions

Every Agent includes a built-in undo system. When the agent creates or modifies data, an **Undo** button appears below its message so you can reverse the action if something is wrong.

### How It Works

- A yellow **Undo** button appears inline after any data-modifying action
- Hover to preview which items will be affected (they highlight in yellow)
- Click to reverse **all actions from that agent turn** (it's all-or-nothing to prevent inconsistent states)
- You can also undo from the **Activity Timeline** on the [Activity](https://app.every.ai/activity) page

### What Can Be Undone

Most data operations can be reversed:

- **Invoices & proposals** — creation, updates, status changes, line item additions
- **Payments & time tracking** — payment records, time entries, expense records
- **Clients & contacts** — creation, updates, contact linking
- **Complex operations** — multi-step workflows, bulk operations, CSV import results

### What Cannot Be Undone

- **Emails already sent** to clients (invoices, notifications)
- **Third-party actions** already processed (Stripe payments, Google Calendar events, HubSpot syncs)
- **Manually edited data** — if you edited the record after the agent action, undo is blocked to protect your changes

### Limitations

- Only the **most recent agent turn** can be undone — once the agent performs a new action, the previous undo buffer is replaced
- After undoing, you cannot "redo" — ask the agent again instead
- If a conflict is detected (manual edits, external processing), the system blocks undo and shows an explanation

<Tip>
  If you notice an error, undo immediately before the agent performs more actions. It is easier to undo right away than after additional turns.
</Tip>

## Activity History

Every action the agent takes is recorded in the **Activity Timeline** on your [Activity](https://app.every.ai/activity) page. Use it to review what the agent did, see which records changed, and undo a past turn even after you've moved on in the conversation.

## Data access and AI limits

Every scopes workspace data to your organization. Connected services use the permissions you grant; for example, Google Drive uses file-by-file access rather than permission to browse your full Drive. Some AI features send the content and context needed for the request to Every's AI service providers, as described in the [Privacy Policy](https://every.ai/privacy).

AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Use the source records, previews, and permission cards to verify consequential work, especially recipient addresses, proposal scope, invoice amounts, payment records, and imported data.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Every Agent send an email or invoice without me reviewing it?">
    In interactive chat, outbound send tools pause on an approval card showing what will be sent. Scheduled and unattended routines can prepare drafts but cannot perform the final send by themselves.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Every replace an accounting system?">
    Every handles proposals, invoices, payments, expenses, time tracking, and operational reporting. It is not a general ledger, bank-reconciliation, payroll, or tax-filing system. Connect Xero or export accounting-ready CSV files when you need those workflows.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a connection give Every access to everything in that service?">
    No. Each connection has a defined permission scope. For example, Google Drive access is limited to files you explicitly select or create with Every. Review the permissions during setup and see the [Privacy Policy](https://every.ai/privacy) for current processing and provider terms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I migrate from another CRM?">
    Yes. Export the source data as CSV or Excel and use Data → Import. Review the Import Agent's mapping before the write, then verify record counts and a sample of clients, invoices, and balances after completion.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
